Privacy

We only process personal data when you write to us, and we count visits only as daily totals. No cookies, no third-party analytics.

Controller

p2cx GmbH, see Imprint · Privacy questions: datenschutz@p2cx.de

Contact and feedback forms

On our pages, for example the home page or handwerk.p2cx.de, you can write to us via a form, sign up for a trial or give feedback.

  • What data: name, email address or phone number, and optionally company, role, number of employees or trade, plus your message. If you arrive via a campaign link, we also store the campaign tag from the address (e.g. utm_campaign). That shows us which ad or post worked.
  • How: The form on handwerk.p2cx.de goes to our server in Germany (IONOS) and is forwarded from there to us as an email. The server does not store the data. The forms on p2cx.de are forwarded to us via a Microsoft service (Azure, data centre in Germany, West Central region). Microsoft acts as our processor. The emails are stored in our mailbox at STRATO GmbH in Berlin.
  • Purpose and legal basis: answering your request and, if you wish, preparing a trial or a collaboration (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Otherwise our legitimate interest in answering requests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: We delete the message once the conversation has ended without a collaboration, at the latest after 12 months. If a contract follows, its terms and the statutory retention periods apply.

Visitor counting without cookies

Like every web server, ours writes a log of requests: page requested, time, browser, referring page and IP address. Once a day we count totals from it: how often each page was viewed, which pages visitors came from and which campaign links they used.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in secure operation and in knowing which content is read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: The server deletes the raw logs with IP addresses after 14 days. For counting, we keep the requests after that without IP addresses, on our own servers in Germany. The daily totals contain no personal data.

Advertising on Google

We run ads in Google Search. If you click an ad, you reach us with a Google identifier (gclid) and our campaign details in the address. We do not use a cookie or a Google tag for this, and we do not report any data about your visit back to Google. What Google itself records when you click is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Cookies and embedded content

No cookies, no third-party analytics, no embedded third-party content. Fonts and scripts are hosted on our own server, so your browser does not request anything from other providers during your visit.

Recipients and third countries

Hosting: IONOS SE, Germany. Email: STRATO GmbH, Berlin. Forms on p2cx.de: Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd., processed in a data centre in Germany. Microsoft belongs to a US group, so access from the US cannot be entirely ruled out. This is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 45 and 46 GDPR). Beyond that, we do not share data with third parties.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. the Independent Centre for Privacy Protection Schleswig-Holstein (ULD).
