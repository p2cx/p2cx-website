Why we don't start with the grand masterplan

Digitalisation only works when the foundations are right and everyone pulls together — IT, data protection, works council, your team. Often what's missing are interfaces to the (somewhat aged) system, or clean data. This is exactly where we bring in our IT and AI know-how: building interfaces, getting data into shape and automating with AI where it genuinely carries its weight. That's why we start small — it's how we get to know each other and create the right conditions together. Honest — instead of a mega-project nobody understands any more.

Getting to know each other protects your budget: No six-figure consulting adventure on spec. The early stages quickly show whether the right people are in place and where the real levers lie. So you only go deep where it's genuinely worth it.
