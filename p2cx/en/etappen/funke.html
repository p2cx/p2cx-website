Pricing — refreshingly clear

You pass on 33% of your savings. No upfront investment, cancel monthly — the 33% runs for as long as the app runs with us, and ends when you cancel.

We calculate the savings together in the workshop and fix them in writing — after that, they're visible to both sides in the KPI dashboard. Workshops, operations, monitoring and the dashboard are all included. Why so affordable? Because we've largely automated the process generation.
