Building Up — your team learns to build

Up to this point, we've built for you. Now we flip it: your people build, we secure. Like getting a driving licence — first lessons, then accompanied driving, then solo. With a safety net that never comes down.

The driving licence — three classes, growing freedom

Nobody drives onto the motorway alone on day one. Your team gets write access at the pace it can safely handle — proven, not felt.

LLearning
Your team builds in the sandbox — free to experiment, nothing reaches production. Anything heading for operations is released by p2cx. Mistakes are explicitly welcome here.
BBacked
Your own leads join the reviews — accompanied building, with p2cx doing spot checks and coaching on real code. The skills move over to you, piece by piece.
FFree
You do your own releases. Only one thing remains: the automatic security gate checks every deploy — forever, for everyone. Including us.
Trust with a net: Every deploy automatically runs through vulnerability, secret and code scans. Red means stop — no matter who pushes. Not a technical fence around your team, but a net underneath it.

What we deliver along the way

Management workshop
Goals, roles, mandate — so the learning has backing from the top.
Training per team
Hands-on, using your real processes — AI-assisted building, clean ways of working with tickets, branches and reviews. No theory slides.
Review & coaching quota
Sparring on real code plus a weekly improvement round — short, regular, honest.

Along the way, your digital operating system takes shape

The most important part of this stage only shows later: while your team learns, the infrastructure that Stage 4 runs on comes into being —

  • Guardrails in the repo: rules and playbooks travel with the code — not in folders nobody reads.
  • Security gates: automatic, permanent, for everyone.
  • Rituals: the improvement round becomes your team's habit.
  • The hardened core: proven templates and standards that everything new builds on.
That's why Building Up and Staying belong together: what's built here keeps running there.

Pricing — predictable instead of project risk

Building Up & Staying run as a fixed monthly rate instead of a project budget — the amount depends on team size and scope and is agreed together before the start. No six-figure consulting adventure, no lock-in contract, adjustable at any time. We'll walk you through the actual numbers in person — depending on how many people are learning and how much we accompany.
